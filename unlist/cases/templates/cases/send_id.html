How this works

  1. Unlist drafts a reply that says the ID copy is attached. You approve it like any other email.
  2. The Subject sends that reply from their own mail app and attaches the copy from their own device. Unlist always hands this one over, even with a live mailbox, and never receives, uploads or stores the document.
  3. The Subject marks it sent. Unlist records only "ID sent" and the date, and the clock runs again.

Before attaching: black out what is not needed

The European Data Protection Board says the person may black out details on an ID copy that are not needed to confirm identity (Guidelines 01/2022, paragraphs 76 and 77), for example the photo, the document's access and serial numbers, nationality and the machine-readable zone. Some national rules require a full copy; the Organization should say so.

The draft says these details were blacked out. If the Subject does not do that, edit the draft before approving it.
