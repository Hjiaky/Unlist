PROTOTYPE, THROWAWAY

What states does a request to an Organization go through?

This demo follows one Case: everything Unlist does for one Subject at one Organization, from the first Access Request to "done". Press buttons and watch the state. The question to answer: are these the right states and the right moves between them, including the awkward cases (no reply, ID demand, refusal, bounce, and mail the Subject has to send by hand)?

Time is simulated in days. "One month to answer" is shown as 30 days plus 3 days' grace. A reminder gives 14 more days. An extension adds 60. Re-checks come after 90 days for a Broker and 365 otherwise. All of these numbers are placeholders to react to.
